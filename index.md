Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2, 2026

1. Introduction
This privacy policy explains how the app ("MyPoly | Vocabulary Builder") handles user data.
The App is designed for language learning and voice‑based practice. It does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect
The App does not collect:
- Personal data
- Audio recordings
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Location data
- Any information that can identify you
No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Use of RECORD_AUDIO Permission
The App requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission solely to enable:
- Voice input
- Pronunciation practice
- Speech‑based interactions inside the App
All audio is processed locally on your device.
No audio is recorded, saved, uploaded, or shared.

4. Third‑Party Services
The App does not use:
- Third‑party analytics
- Advertising SDKs
- Cloud storage
- External APIs that receive user data

5. Children’s Privacy
The App does not collect personal information from children or adults. It is safe for all ages.

6. Data Security
Because the App does not collect or store data, no personal information is at risk. All processing happens on the user’s device.

7. Changes to This Policy
If the App’s functionality changes in the future, this privacy policy will be updated. Users will be notified of any significant changes.
